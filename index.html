About

Our mission

Strate is an independent tech publication founded by a senior software engineer with over a decade of expertise in scalable cloud architectures, high-stakes web deployment, and AI-driven systems.

Our conviction: The European tech landscape is at a critical crossroads. While mainstream media focuses on venture capital and valuations, the technical foundations — infrastructure resilience, deployment velocity, and digital sovereignty — remain largely overlooked by traditional outlets.

Strate bridges this gap. We provide a "deployment-first" perspective on innovation, analyzing how complex technologies actually reach production. We don't cover tech for the hype; we analyze the stacks that power the modern digital economy.

Our editorial pillars:

  • ▸Enterprise Scalability & SaaS — Deconstructing the architectures that allow high-growth startups to scale into global tech leaders.
  • ▸Applied AI & Machine Learning — Beyond the benchmarks: analyzing the engineering challenges of integrating AI into real-world industrial and commercial workflows.
  • ▸Infrastructure & Security — Monitoring the shift toward sovereign clouds, edge computing, and zero-trust security standards.

Strate is an independent editorial project. No sponsored content, no equity stakes. Our only commitment is to technical rigour.
