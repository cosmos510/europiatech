Editorial Focus

We publish long-form technical analysis — not press releases. Our three editorial pillars:

  • ▸AI in Production — European AI labs, SLMs, open-source models, AI Act compliance. How AI is actually deployed, not just benchmarked.
  • ▸Infrastructure & Sovereignty — Sovereign cloud, European datacenters, GDPR-compliant architectures, edge computing.

Every piece is written with an engineer's rigour: verified sources, architecture-level understanding, and a critical eye on marketing claims.

Why We Cover Events

EuropiaTech attends conferences to produce content that outlasts the event itself:

  • ▸Technical interviews — We sit down with CTOs and engineering leads to discuss architecture decisions, not product pitches.
  • ▸In-depth analysis — Post-event articles that break down announcements with real technical context.
  • ▸Pre-event previews — We publish reading grids before the event so our audience arrives prepared.

Our coverage reaches a highly targeted technical audience that generic tech media doesn't serve. We don't do recaps — we do analysis.
