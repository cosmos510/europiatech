Preview: 3 Key Themes to Watch at Tech Conferences in 2026

February 8, 2026 · By Maxime Martin, Lead Analyst

This year's tech conferences no longer resemble the demo showcases of the past. The focus has shifted from "gadgets" to resilience and sovereignty. Here's what the Strate team will be watching closely.

Theme 1

AI & Industrial Cybersecurity Convergence

The integration of AI at the heart of SCADA systems and critical infrastructure represents a paradigm shift. Industrial systems — power plants, water distribution networks, production lines — have long operated in isolation. Their progressive connection to IP networks exposes them to attack vectors they were never designed for.

AI steps in here as a real-time anomaly detection layer. A model trained on the normal behaviour of an industrial controller can identify a deviation — a frequency change, an unusual command — before it turns into an incident.

▸ We'll be looking for real-world use cases of AI-driven anomaly detection in the field, not lab demonstrations.

Theme 2

Cloud Sovereignty Beyond GDPR

The debate is shifting towards technical autonomy. GDPR laid the legal foundations, but real sovereignty requires more than regulatory compliance. It demands technical independence from American hyperscalers — AWS, Azure, GCP — whose infrastructures are subject to the Cloud Act and extraterritorial jurisdictions.

Which European solutions enable full independence today? OVHcloud, Scaleway, Clever Cloud, Infomaniak offer credible alternatives, but the question of feature parity remains open. The managed services (databases, AI, analytics) of hyperscalers don't yet have European equivalents at the same scale.

▸ We'll interview sovereign cloud players on-site to concretely assess their technical maturity.

Theme 3

Energy at the Service of Compute

With the explosion of compute demands for AI, datacenter energy efficiency is becoming a major technical selling point. An AI training cluster consumes as much as a small town. The question is no longer "how many GPUs?" but "how many watts per token?"

Direct-to-chip liquid cooling is progressively replacing traditional air conditioning. Immersion cooling solutions — where servers are submerged in dielectric fluid — enable compute densities impossible with air cooling. Intelligent load management, coupled with renewable energy sources, is becoming a competitive differentiator for European datacenter operators.

▸ We'll analyse the liquid cooling and load management solutions presented, with real PUE figures.

Why We'll Be There

Strate will cover these themes with a strictly technical angle, far from the hype, to provide decision-makers with an actionable reading grid. No reformulated press releases — technical interviews, architecture analysis, and an engineer's critical eye on every announcement.
